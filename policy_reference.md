# Policy reference used by the tool

Source: Vireo Audio Customer Support Operating Policy v3.2.

Relevant rules:
- First-response targets: chat 15m, voice callback 2h, social 4h, email 8h.
- Missed first-response target creates a Rs 350 store credit.
- Replacement planning cost = product unit cost + Rs 340 logistics.
- Tier 2 cases are multi-touch and are not to be compared with Tier 1 on volume metrics.
- CSAT blanks are excluded from averages; auto-closed tickets count as completed attendance.
- Helpdesk timestamps are IST; legacy resolution timestamps were reconstructed from a UTC legacy event log.
- Handle time = first response to resolution.
